PETROLHEADS – TIER 3 SERVICES

DIAGNOSTICS & MAINTENANCE

1. WHY TIER 3 SERVICES EXIST

Tier 3 services are not meant to compete with local garages or mass-service aggregators. They position Petrolheads as an engineering-first automotive consultant, allowing structured vehicle understanding and customer education.

2. ADVANCED DIAGNOSTIC SERVICES

2.1 What You Will Do

  • Full ECU diagnostics and fault code analysis
  • Live sensor data review
  • Brake, suspension, and steering inspection
  • Electrical and battery health assessment
  • Test drive for NVH and drivability evaluation

2.2 How to Do Diagnostics Smartly

  • Always charge for diagnostics
  • Provide a written diagnostic health report
  • Use findings to educate customers
  • Recommend phased solutions instead of pushing sales

2.3 Advantages of Diagnostics

  • High trust and credibility
  • Low consumable costs
  • Strong gateway to higher-tier services

2.4 Disadvantages & Risks

  • Customer may take the report elsewhere
  • Requires skilled interpretation
  • Time-consuming if poorly managed

2.5 Profit & Loss Scenarios (Diagnostics)

Profit Scenario:

  • High-margin standalone service
  • Converts into Tier 2 or Tier 1 projects

Loss Scenario:

  • One-time customers only

Risk Control:

  • Proper pricing
  • Position report as Petrolheads intellectual property

3. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE SERVICES

3.1 What You Will Do

  • Engine oil, brake fluid, coolant replacement
  • Air, oil, and cabin filter replacement
  • Brake servicing
  • Suspension wear inspection
  • Long-drive reliability checks

3.2 How to Do Maintenance Smartly

  • Sell maintenance as packages
  • Avoid price-based competition
  • Focus on reliability and longevity
  • Refer heavy repairs externally

3.3 Advantages of Maintenance Services

  • Repeat customer visits
  • Predictable revenue flow

3.4 Disadvantages & Risks

  • Labour dependency
  • Lower margins than premium services

3.5 Profit & Loss Scenarios (Maintenance)

Profit Scenario:

  • Package-based pricing
  • OEM+ consumables

Loss Scenario:

  • Competing on price with local garages

Risk Control:

  • Maintain premium positioning

4. DOs & DON’Ts FOR TIER 3

DO:

  • Document every inspection
  • Educate customers
  • Use Tier 3 as a feeder to premium services

DON’T:

  • Offer free diagnostics
  • Become a mass-service centre

5. FINAL POSITIONING

Tier 3 services are strategic enablers, not profit centres alone. Their true value lies in trust, data, and converting it into higher-margin engineering services.
