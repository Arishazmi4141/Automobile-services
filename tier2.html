PETROLHEADS – TIER 2 SERVICES

AESTHETICS & SAFETY

1. WHY TIER 2 SERVICES EXIST

Tier 2 services are where customers consciously choose to spend more because the value is visible, tangible, and immediately experienced. Aesthetics and safety upgrades satisfy emotional needs (pride, confidence) while remaining rational purchases, making them ideal for sustainable business growth.

2. AESTHETICS SERVICES

2.1 Scope of Aesthetic Services

  • Premium ceramic and graphene coatings
  • Paint Protection Film (PPF)
  • Exterior paint enhancement and correction
  • Deep interior detailing and leather treatment
  • Engine bay detailing

2.2 Smart Execution Strategy – Aesthetics

  • Sell the outcome (finish, durability, ease of cleaning), not the chemical
  • Maintain strict surface preparation standards
  • Offer packages instead of discounts
  • Limit daily car intake to protect quality

2.3 Advantages of Aesthetics Services

  • Strong visual marketing impact
  • Easy referrals and social media visibility

2.4 Disadvantages & Risks – Aesthetics

  • Labour-intensive
  • Warranty disputes if the preparation is poor
  • Quality consistency challenges

2.5 Profit & Loss Scenarios – Aesthetics

Profit Scenario:

  • Premium materials and disciplined workflow

Loss Scenario:

  • Underpricing and rushing jobs

Risk Mitigation:

  • Fewer jobs, higher standards

3. SAFETY & CONFIDENCE SERVICES

3.1 Scope of Safety Services

  • Brake upgrade programs (pads, fluids, lines)
  • Tyre selection and upgrade consultation
  • Suspension safety upgrades (OEM+)
  • Alignment, balancing, and stability optimisation
  • Visibility and lighting upgrades (legal)

3.2 Smart Execution Strategy – Safety

  • Recommend upgrades only when genuinely required
  • Document inspections and improvements

3.3 Advantages of Safety Services

  • Builds strong customer trust
  • Repeat business and long-term loyalty
  • Lower legal and compliance risk

3.4 Disadvantages & Risks – Safety

  • High responsibility and liability
  • Requires skilled labour and quality parts

4. DOs & DON’Ts FOR TIER 2

DO:

  • Maintain premium positioning
  • Document every job
  • Educate customers on safety and longevity

DON’T:

  • Compete on the lowest price
  • Promise unrealistic outcomes
  • Compromise on safety

5. SHOULD YOU DO TIER 2 SERVICES?

Why You SHOULD:

  • Strong revenue and brand visibility
  • Scalable with trained teams

Why You Should Be Careful:

  • Quality failures damage reputation quickly
  • Overexpansion reduces consistency
